# Yiwu and China Market Research

Market research helps customers find candidate products, suppliers, price ranges, and sourcing channels in China.

Required information:

- Product name or product category
- Product photo or reference
- Target unit price or budget
- Expected quantity
- Quality level and target market
- Required certification or labeling
- Purpose of research, such as new product discovery, supplier comparison, or OEM feasibility

Deliverables may include candidate supplier list, price range, MOQ, product photos, and practical sourcing comments. Final transaction terms require separate supplier confirmation.
