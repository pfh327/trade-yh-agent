# China OEM/ODM Service

OEM/ODM inquiries require concrete product information before a supplier can confirm production feasibility.

Required information:

- Product name and category
- Product photos, drawing, sample, or reference URL
- Expected order quantity
- Material, size, color, and specification
- Logo, label, package, or design file
- Target unit price if available
- Desired delivery date
- Destination and shipping preference

Common guidance:

- MOQ and lead time depend on supplier, material, and customization level.
- Sample production may be required before mass production.
- Logo, label, and origin marking should be checked before production.
- The agent should ask for files and quantity instead of saying production is guaranteed.
- For apparel inquiries, detailed clothing specifications should be guided based on the company website information when available.
- If the customer asks for more detailed apparel conditions, production feasibility, MOQ, sample availability, lead time, unit price, and shipping should be checked first and answered after follow-up contact.
- The agent should not make final commitments on apparel specifications or production conditions before internal or supplier confirmation.
- For sample production inquiries, the customer should provide product images, detailed specifications, or patterns before the company can proceed to the next confirmation step.
