# China Purchasing Agency Service

Purchasing agency inquiries often start from a product URL on Chinese platforms such as 1688, Alibaba, Taobao, or a supplier catalog.

Required information:

- Product URL or supplier name
- Option, color, size, model, or specification
- Quantity
- Destination
- Inspection requirement
- Packaging and labeling requirement
- Desired schedule

Process:

1. Check product and supplier information.
2. Confirm unit price, domestic China shipping fee, MOQ, stock, and lead time.
3. Confirm inspection, packaging, and international shipping conditions.
4. Provide estimated quotation after supplier confirmation.

The agent must avoid final price guarantees until supplier confirmation.
