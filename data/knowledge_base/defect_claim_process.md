# Defect and Claim Response

Defect and damage cases require evidence and transaction history. The agent should not promise refund or replacement before reviewing evidence and supplier terms.

Required information:

- Defect or damage photos and videos
- Total received quantity
- Defective quantity and defect rate
- Date of receipt
- Order sheet, invoice, contract, chat record, or inspection report
- Packaging condition
- Whether inspection was performed before shipment

Recommended process:

1. Collect evidence.
2. Compare defect details against order specification.
3. Check inspection and acceptance history.
4. Prepare claim message to supplier.
5. Negotiate replacement, discount, refund, or next-order compensation when possible.

Risk note:

Supplier responsibility depends on contract terms, evidence quality, inspection result, and logistics damage possibility.
