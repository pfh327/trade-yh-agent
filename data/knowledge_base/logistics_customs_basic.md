# Logistics, Customs, Labeling, and Certification

Logistics and customs guidance depends on product category, material, HS code, use case, quantity, destination, and regulatory requirements.

Important checks:

- Product category and material
- Quantity, weight, and volume
- Shipping method: air, sea, express, or consolidated cargo
- Destination address
- Invoice, packing list, and origin information
- Origin marking and Korean label requirements
- KC or other certification requirement where applicable

The agent may provide an initial checklist but must recommend expert or customs-broker confirmation for final customs and certification decisions.
